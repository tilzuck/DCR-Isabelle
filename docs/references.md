# References

in the theories are also in `document/root.bib`, under the same keys, for the
bibliography of the session document. Bibliographic data comes from the
literature survey this project started with. Entries marked † still need their
details (DOI, pages) checked.

## Other formalisations and libraries

| Key | Reference |
|---|---|
| [AFP23] | A. Christfort, S. Debois. *DCR Syntax and Execution Equivalent Markings.* Archive of Formal Proofs, 2023. https://isa-afp.org/entries/DCR-ExecutionEquivalence.html. An Isabelle formalisation of DCR graphs with five relations and finite runs; it proves that execution-equivalent markings preserve accepting runs. The only existing proof-assistant formalisation of DCR graphs we found |
